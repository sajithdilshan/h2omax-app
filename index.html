A plan from your first sip.

Small sips, at the right time.

Choose a daily goal and when you want to be done. Your first drink starts the day, and H2OMax spreads the rest into easy amounts, with a gentle reminder whenever the next sip is due. Ahead of plan? It eases off. Fallen behind? It catches you up a little at a time, never all at once.

Meet Puff.

Puff is a pufferfish who lives in your pool. Every drink raises the water and puffs Puff up a little more, so a glance tells you how your day is going. Reach your goal day after day and Puff's streak grows. Miss one, and Puff will need some care to get well again.
