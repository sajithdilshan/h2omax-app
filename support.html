Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone and Apple Watch models and iOS / watchOS versions.

My drinks aren't showing in Apple Health

Open the Health app, tap your profile picture → Apps → H2OMax, and make sure Water is turned on under both "Allow to Write" and "Allow to Read". You can also check the status in H2OMax → Settings → Apple Health.

I'm not getting reminders

Check Settings → Notifications → H2OMax on your iPhone, and any Focus modes that might be silencing it. Reminders start once your day has started (your first drink, or the Start button) and stop when you reach your goal or pass your cutoff.

When does a new day start?

At 4:00 in the morning, so a late-night glass still counts toward the day you were in.

Why does Puff look unwell?

Puff gets a little sicker for each day you miss your goal before your cutoff, and heals as soon as you hit it again. Keep a streak going to unlock rewards at 7, 30 and 100 days.

The watch isn't up to date

Open H2OMax on your iPhone once with your watch nearby. Drinks logged on the watch while your phone is away are saved and sent over the next time they connect.
