Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

H2OMax is a water-tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes. The short version: we don't collect any of your data. Everything stays on your devices.

Data we collect

None. H2OMax has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet. The developer cannot see your data.

Data stored on your devices

To work, the app stores your settings (daily goal, cutoff time, reminder interval, quick-add amounts), today's drinks and Puff's streak on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Some of this is shared with the H2OMax widgets and watch complication on the same device so they can show your progress. Deleting the app removes this data.

Apple Health

With your permission, H2OMax saves each drink you log to Apple Health as dietary water, and reads your dietary water from Apple Health to show your progress and history. Health data is used only inside the app to provide these features. It is never sent to the developer or any third party, never used for advertising or data mining, and never stored in iCloud by H2OMax. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

iPhone and Apple Watch sync

Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange your progress and settings directly with each other using Apple's Watch Connectivity framework. This data doesn't pass through any server operated by the developer.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

Children

H2OMax doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the H2OMax support page on GitHub.
